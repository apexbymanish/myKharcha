# Where to submit an iOS app

Ordered by what is realistic for a free, independent app with no press contacts.
Verify every address on the site before sending — tip lines move, and a bounced
pitch is a wasted one.

---

## 1. Apple itself (do this first)

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Featuring Nomination | App Store Connect → your app → **Featuring** → Nominate |
| App Store editorial guidance | https://developer.apple.com/app-store/getting-featured/ |
| Marketing resources & badges | https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/ |

Free, twenty minutes, read by Apple's editors, and almost nobody submits.
Draft is in `featuring-nomination.md`.

---

## 2. Apple-focused publications

These review iOS apps as a matter of routine. Small, independent apps do get
covered — a short, specific pitch with a promo code works far better than a
press release.

| Outlet | Site | How to reach them |
|---|---|---|
| **MacStories** | https://www.macstories.net | Contact page; best fit for a well-designed indie app |
| **9to5Mac** | https://9to5mac.com | https://9to5mac.com/tips/ |
| **MacRumors** | https://www.macrumors.com | https://www.macrumors.com/contact/ |
| **Cult of Mac** | https://www.cultofmac.com | Contact / tips page |
| **AppAdvice** | https://appadvice.com | App submission form on site |
| **Beautiful Pixels** | https://beautifulpixels.com | Design-led; strong fit for this app |
| **The Sweet Setup** | https://thesweetsetup.com | Covers "best app for X" roundups |
| **iGeeksBlog** | https://www.igeeksblog.com | India-based Apple site — good fit for the Hindi listing |

**MacStories and Beautiful Pixels are the two worth the most effort here.** Both
cover independent, design-conscious apps and both have written up smaller apps
than this one.

## 3. Directories and listings (backlinks + long-tail search)

These rarely send traffic on day one, but they rank, and people genuinely search
"free alternative to X".

| Site | Why |
|---|---|
| https://alternativeto.net | Ranks well for "alternative to" searches; list yourself against the paid trackers |
| https://www.producthunt.com | One-day spike, permanent backlink |
| https://www.slant.co | "Best expense tracker" style comparison lists |
| https://appshopper.com | Long-standing App Store directory |
| https://www.indieappscatalogue.com | Indie-only, curated |
| https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html | Show HN guidelines |

## How to pitch

Keep it to five sentences. Editors read the first two.

> Subject: Paisa Khoi? — free expense tracker, paste a bank SMS, 20 languages
>
> Paisa Khoi? is a free iPhone expense tracker with no subscription, no ads and
> no account. Paste a bank message or receipt and it reads the amount, date and
> category on device — nothing is uploaded. It works in any currency and is
> fully translated into twenty languages, including Nepali, Bengali, Tamil and
> Urdu, which most finance apps skip.
>
> App Store: <link>
> Screenshots: <link>
> Happy to answer anything — I built it.

Attach nothing. Link to screenshots rather than embedding them. Offer a promo
code only if the app is paid — this one is free, which is itself the story.
